Invitation to Participate in a Semi-Structured Interview

Dear Participant,

I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to cordially invite you to participate in a semi-structured interview as part of the doctoral research project titled “Micro-credentials to Enhance Pedagogical Competence for Pre-service English Teachers of a Public University in Northern Mexico”, conducted through the School of Philosophy and Arts at the Universidad Autónoma de Nuevo León.

The purpose of this interview is to gain in-depth insights into the perceptions of 10th-semester students in the Applied Linguistics Program (Licenciatura en Lingüística Aplicada a la Enseñanza y Traducción del Inglés) regarding pedagogical, linguistic, and intercultural competencies.

Participation Details:

  • Format: Online one-on-one semi-structured interview.
  • Estimated Duration: Approximately 30 minutes.
  • Voluntary Nature: Completely voluntary. You may choose not to answer specific questions or withdraw at any time without penalty.

Confidentiality and Ethical Standards:

  • All records will be kept strictly confidential and anonymized using pseudonyms or numerical identifiers.
  • Data will be stored securely in encrypted, password-protected files accessible only to the research team.
  • Participation poses no more risk than those encountered in daily life.

Thank you in advance for considering this invitation and for your valuable contribution to this research project. Please select your time slot through our official booking portal.

Respectfully,

MIE. Jesús Eduardo Doria Navarro
